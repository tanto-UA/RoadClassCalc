Junction density is defined as the average number of intersections or junctions per kilometre along the road segment:
  • High (> 3 per km): Typically urban routes with high frequency of cross streets. (VW = +1)
  • Moderate (≤ 3 per km): Inter-urban roads, highways, or suburban routes with spaced-out junctions. (VW = 0)

Ambient luminance describes the background brightness of the surrounding environment, affecting visual adaptation:
  • High (VW = +1): Intense background light (e.g. bright shop fronts, sports fields, large billboards).
  • Moderate (VW = 0): Standard baseline environments (e.g. typical residential streets, suburban areas).
  • Low (VW = -1): Dark, rural, or isolated areas with minimal background lighting.

Difficulty of the navigational task measures the mental effort and attention required to navigate safely:
  • Very Difficult (VW = +2): Highly complex road geometry, lack of visual guidance, multiple conflicts.
  • Difficult (VW = +1): Active attention needed (e.g. standard roundabouts, urban streets, high signage).
  • Easy (VW = 0): Simple, predictable layout (e.g. straight roads, clear lane markings).
